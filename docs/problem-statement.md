# Problem Statement

## Background

Campus canteens serve a large number of students and staff, especially during short break and lunch periods. During peak hours, the traditional counter-based ordering process becomes difficult to manage because many students need to place their orders at the same time.

## The Problem

Students have to stand in long queues to view the menu, place food orders, and wait for their food during busy campus hours. Manual order taking is time-consuming and can result in order mistakes.

The absence of an online pre-order facility makes it difficult for students to order food in advance and collect it conveniently.

## Who is Affected

Students

Spend time waiting in queues.
Face delays during short breaks.
Have limited convenience when ordering food.

Canteen Staff

Handle orders manually.
Need to manage multiple orders during peak hours.
May face difficulties in tracking and organizing orders.

College/University

Experiences crowded canteen areas.
Has an inefficient manual ordering process.
Needs better digital management of orders and records.

## Why It Matters

Long queues and manual ordering reduce the efficiency of campus food services. Students may lose valuable break time waiting for food, while canteen staff have to handle a large number of orders manually.

A digital pre-ordering system can make the process faster and more organized by allowing students to view the menu, select items, place orders in advance, and track their orders.

## Why Existing Solutions Fall Short

The traditional canteen process relies on manual ordering and counter-based service. Students must physically visit the counter to place their orders, and staff must manually handle and track those orders.

While a normal canteen system provides food ordering, it does not provide a dedicated campus-based online pre-order workflow with features such as digital menu viewing, advance ordering, order tracking, and centralized order management.

Our Campus Canteen Pre-Order System addresses this gap by providing a web-based platform for students and canteen administrators.
